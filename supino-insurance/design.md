# Supino Insurance: design system (v4, editorial)
Rebuilt 2026-10-08 after the owner said v3 (GEICO-style blue/yellow, stock families, cartoon cars) looked AI-made.

**Premise:** Good coverage, from people you can call by name. A real family agency, not a brand.
**Reference:** Chubb (chubb.com): serif headlines, one calm photo, lots of ivory space, almost no color. Borrowed the serif + italic accent word and the quiet hairline-divided layout. No Chubb assets.
