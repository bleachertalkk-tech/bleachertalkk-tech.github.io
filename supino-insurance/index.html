          Just had an accident?

          1. Get safe. Move out of traffic if you can. Call the police and report any injuries.
          2. Swap details. Year, make, model and plate. Driver's name, address, license number and insurance company. Names of passengers and witnesses.
          3. Take photos. The cars, the damage, the street, the signs and any skid marks.
          4. Call us. We'll help you report it and tell you what happens next.

          Our story

          A Malden family agency, now in its second generation.

          Joseph M. Supino grew up in Everett, served as an Army lieutenant, and worked at Hearthstone Insurance and Allstate. In 1989 he bought a small Malden agency, and his son Michael joined him to run it.

          Michael bought the agency in 1997 and renamed it Supino Insurance Agency. He opened the Lynnfield office in 2008. Joe coached youth football and served as Lynnfield's Recreation Commissioner. He passed away in February 2025. Michael runs the agency today.
