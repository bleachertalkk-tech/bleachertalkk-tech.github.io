Questions

What owners ask me.

Why would you build it for free?

Because showing beats telling. Most owners can't picture a new site until they see their own. If you like it, you keep it. If not, no hard feelings.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. No card, no contract, nothing to sign until you've seen your site and want it live.

What if I don't like it?

Tell me what to change and I'll change it. If it's still not right, you walk away and owe nothing.

Do I need a website already?

No. Plenty of businesses only have a Google listing. I build the site from that.

I already have a website. Can you still help?

Yes. If yours is old, slow, or hard to use on a phone, I'll build a new one and you can compare them side by side.

Who owns the website?

Your business name, logo, photos and info are always yours. Any web address is registered in your business's name. If you buy the site outright, all of it is yours.

How do changes work?

On the care plan, text me what you want changed. Hours, prices, photos, specials and wording are included.

Policies

Cancellation, refunds and payments

Cancellation

The care plan is month to month. Cancel anytime by text or email before your next billing date, and you won't be charged again. Your site stays up until the end of the month you paid for.

Refunds

You see your website before you pay. If something isn't right before launch, I'll fix it. The setup fee, a month that has already started, and the one-time $800 purchase are not refundable once the site is live. Billing mistakes are always refunded in full.

Payments

All payments are by card through Stripe's secure checkout. I never see or store your card number. You get an email receipt for every charge.

Delivery

Your site goes live within 7 days of signing, after you confirm your info (phone, hours, services) is right. Changes on the care plan are usually done within 2 business days.
