Policies

Cancellation, refunds and payments.

Cancellation

The care plan is month to month. Cancel anytime by text or email before your next billing date, and you won't be charged again. Your site stays up until the end of the month you paid for.

Refunds

You see your website before you pay. If something isn't right before launch, I'll fix it. The setup fee, a month that has already started, and the one-time $800 purchase are not refundable once the site is live. Billing mistakes are always refunded in full.

Payments

All payments are by card through Stripe's secure checkout. I never see or store your card number. You get an email receipt for every charge.

Delivery

Your site goes live within 7 days of signing, after you confirm your info (phone, hours, services) is right. Changes on the care plan are usually done within 2 business days.
